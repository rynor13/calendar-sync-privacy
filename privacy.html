Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page covers the personal Google Apps Script project "Outlook to Google Calendar Sync" ("the script").

What it does

The script reads a single, privately published calendar feed (an ICS link) from the owner's own Outlook calendar and writes matching event titles and times into a calendar in the owner's own Google account. It runs automatically on a schedule under the owner's Google account.

Data collection and sharing

The script does not collect, store, sell, or share any data with third parties. All calendar data it reads and writes stays entirely within the owner's own Google account and Google Apps Script project. No data is transmitted to any external server, service, or person other than Google's own infrastructure running the script.

Scope of use

This script is for personal, single-user use only and is not distributed, published, or made available to the public.
